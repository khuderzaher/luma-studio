Brand identity / visual study

Soft forms.
Clear voice.

A study in balancing softness with a confident, unmistakable point of view.

FICTIONAL CONCEPT STUDY · 2026
DisciplineBrand identity
ScopeStrategy, identity, typography, color
Studio roleConcept & art direction
Project typeIndependent study

01 / The question

How can softness feel distinctive?

The brief imagines a contemporary lifestyle brand whose warmth never slips into the generic. The visual system starts from an editorial wordmark, a gently unexpected scale relationship, and a palette that allows tenderness and contrast to coexist.

Typography is treated as a material, not decoration. The study moves from a quiet page to expressive scale, testing how a consistent identity can feel alive across packaging, print, and digital touchpoints.

02 / A visual language

holds a feeling.

Color and composition are shown as design explorations, not approved client assets.

03 / What it explores

Built to be recognisable.

The concept tests how a visual idea can flex across formats while keeping a clear point of view. It prioritises rhythm, material contrast, and the little details that give a system its character.

Because this is a fictional exercise, the page presents intention and design decisions rather than fabricated outcomes, performance metrics, or client testimonials.
