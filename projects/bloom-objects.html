01 / The question

How can everyday objects feel worth keeping?

Bloom is a fictional home-object label built around material honesty and small routines. The art direction draws from warm clay, paper, soft shadows, and shapes that feel as if they were found rather than manufactured for a campaign.

The visual system gives the product room to breathe. Packaging notes, generous framing, and considered typography connect the touchpoints without forcing every composition into the same layout.

03 / What it explores

Built to be recognisable.

The concept tests how a visual idea can flex across formats while keeping a clear point of view. It prioritises rhythm, material contrast, and the little details that give a system its character.

Because this is a fictional exercise, the page presents intention and design decisions rather than fabricated outcomes, performance metrics, or client testimonials.
