STUDIO NOTES / 01

Make it mean

An approach, not a formula

Make the right thing feel inevitable.

Some projects need a quiet voice. Others need to take up space. We start by looking closely — at the idea, the people it’s for, and the details that make it worth noticing.

Then we build a visual language around those truths. No default aesthetic. No design for design’s sake. Just a clear point of view, carried through with care.

Luma is a fictional studio concept created to demonstrate this approach. The projects in this site are explorations, not client commissions.
